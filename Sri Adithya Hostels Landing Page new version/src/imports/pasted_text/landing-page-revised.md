What Changed & Why
Three things drove the hero rejection:

Too much text fighting for attention in one zone
The live dashboard card on the right felt clever but cold
It lost the warmth the original had

The fix: keep the original hero's breathing room and emotional tone, but upgrade what's in each zone. Less copy, more humanity. Owner's face + real hostel imagery does more conversion work than any headline ever will.

Right Column — The Big Upgrade
This is where the redesign fundamentally changes from what was built.
No placeholder box. No dashboard cards. Two real elements stacked.
Top element — Owner Photo Card:
A warm card (soft ivory background, saffron left-border accent, 16px rounded corners). Inside:

Left: Srinivasa Rao's half-body photo — circular crop, slightly overlapping the card edge for a natural feel, not a passport photo feel
Right of photo: His name in navy semibold — "Srinivasa Rao", below that in gray small text — "Owner, Sri Adithya Hostels", below that one line in saffron italic — "Here to help you find your home near SNIST."
Bottom of card: Two micro CTA icons — 📞 small call icon + 💬 small WhatsApp icon, both tappable

Why this works: The single highest trust signal on any service business page is a real human face with a name. Parents especially respond to this. It says: there is a real accountable person here, not a faceless property.
Bottom element — Auto-Scrolling Hostel Image Strip:
Below the owner card, a horizontal strip showing 3 hostel photo placeholders that auto-scroll every 3 seconds. Each placeholder is a rounded card (same size, uniform). On hover, scrolling pauses. Below the strip, small dot navigation indicators — 3 dots, active dot is saffron.
When real photos arrive: room photo, food photo, building exterior — these three slots. Until then, warm gradient placeholders with subtle labels: "Room Interior", "Daily Meals", "Hostel Building" — so the owner understands exactly what photos to send for each slot.
Why this works: Auto-scroll creates a sense of life and activity without requiring any interaction. It communicates "this is a real, active place" even with placeholders.
