Design a modern mobile-first web application for a hostel and PG management platform called NIVĀ.

NIVĀ is a centralized operational platform built for hostel owners and PG managers to manage multiple hostels efficiently through one system. The platform helps owners manage tenants, rooms, occupancy, finances, expenses, communication, move-outs, analytics, and business operations digitally instead of relying on spreadsheets, paperwork, and manual tracking.

The product should feel:

* operational
* trustworthy
* scalable
* modern
* investor-ready
* organized
* clean and efficient

This is a mobile-first web application, not a desktop SaaS dashboard.

The design should prioritize:

* quick decision making
* fast workflows
* operational visibility
* mobile usability
* thumb-friendly interactions
* clean hierarchy
* modular layouts
* quick scanning
* structured information architecture

Primary users:

* hostel owners
* PG managers
* accommodation operators

Visual inspiration:

* Stripe
* Linear
* Notion
* modern B2B SaaS products
* operational dashboards

Avoid:

* flashy startup gradients
* excessive glassmorphism
* crypto dashboard aesthetics
* cluttered interfaces
* overly playful visuals
* excessive neon colors

Use:

* clean typography hierarchy
* modular card systems
* rounded corners
* subtle shadows
* structured spacing
* whitespace
* soft surfaces
* collapsible analytics sections where needed

Color palette:

* midnight navy (#111827)
* teal accent (#14B8A6)
* white surfaces
* soft gray backgrounds
* semantic colors for alerts, overdue payments, and occupancy statuses

Typography:

* modern sans-serif
* clean and highly readable
* medium-to-bold hierarchy
* optimized for mobile readability

GLOBAL APPLICATION STRUCTURE:

Use a bottom navigation bar for:

* Portfolio
* Hostels
* Alerts
* Billing & Plans
* Settings

The application has two major layers:

1. Portfolio Layer
   A global business overview where owners can manage all hostels together.

2. Hostel Workspace Layer
   A dedicated operational workspace for an individual hostel.

PORTFOLIO DASHBOARD:

The portfolio dashboard should provide a summarized business overview across all hostels without overwhelming the user with too many graphs.

Include:

* total revenue
* total occupancy
* pending dues
* total tenants
* total hostels
* monthly expenses
* total vacancies

Add:

* combined occupancy trend graph
* combined revenue analytics
* smart business insight cards
* alerts section
* hostel comparison cards

Business insight examples:

* highest occupancy hostel
* overdue payment alerts
* expected move-outs
* academic mix insights
* future vacancy predictions

Each hostel card should include:

* hostel name
* occupancy percentage
* overdue amount
* vacant rooms
* monthly revenue

Add a highly visible primary CTA:

* Create New Hostel

The portfolio dashboard should feel like a business control center for hostel operations.

HOSTELS SECTION:

This section should allow owners to:

* view all hostels
* create new hostels
* search hostels
* filter hostels
* manage hostel information
* quickly access hostel workspaces

Each hostel card should display:

* occupancy percentage
* active tenants
* vacant rooms
* revenue
* overdue dues

INDIVIDUAL HOSTEL WORKSPACE:

When an owner selects a hostel, they enter a dedicated operational workspace for that hostel.

Inside the hostel workspace use top tabs or segmented navigation:

* Overview
* Rooms
* Tenants
* Financials
* Expenses
* Move-Outs
* Activity Log
* Settings

FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT:

The financial section should help owners manage all payment operations.

Include:

* expected monthly collection
* received payments
* pending dues
* overdue payments
* overdue recovered

Add:

* payment analytics
* online vs offline payment breakdown
* payment history
* recent transactions

Use semantic colors for:

* overdue
* pending

The financial system should feel secure, operational, and trustworthy.

MOVE-OUT MANAGEMENT:

The move-out section should help owners manage future vacancies and tenant exits.

Include:

* move-out requests
* expected leaving dates
* room numbers
* pending dues
* move-out status tags

When a tenant submits a move-out request:

* the owner receives a notification
* the tenant profile receives a “Move-Out Notified” tag
* the room becomes marked as a future vacancy

Add:

* future vacancy forecasting
* occupancy impact insights
* academic mix analytics
* expected churn analysis

The overall product should feel like a modern operational control center for hostel businesses across India with strong UX clarity, structured workflows, scalable architecture, and clean investor-ready design.
